## Chapter 2 — To derive next

First vary the metric-compatible contortion to derive the Cartan spin–torsion equation, then vary the metric to obtain the gravitational metric equation. Only after those steps introduce a consistent Weyssenhoff spin fluid, eliminate torsion, specialize to collapse geometry, and test the bounce conditions.

**Convention:** The covariant derivative is `\nabla_\mu V^\rho=\partial_\mu V^\rho+\Gamma^\rho{}_{\mu\nu}V^\nu`; the full connection is `\widetilde\Gamma=\{g\}+K`. Other literature may use a different contortion sign and a factor of two in torsion.
